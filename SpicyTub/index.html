Our Heritage

Where Every Bite Tells a Story

Welcome to SpicyTub, where the regal heritage of Indian culinary craft meets contemporary dining sophistication. Born from a reverence for ancient spice routes, hand-ground masalas, and patient slow-fire techniques, our kitchen celebrates the glorious breadth of Indian food culture.

From the fragrant saffron dum pots of Hyderabad to the sun-soaked mustard fields of Punjab and the coastal pepper groves of Malabar, every recipe is hand-crafted with pure desi ghee, organic whole spices, and farm-fresh ingredients. At SpicyTub, we do not merely serve food—we craft unforgettable memories around the dining table.
